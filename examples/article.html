Getting around Harbour City

Most of the city is close to a bus. According to Harbour City Transport, 91% of households live within 400 metres of a bus stop.

Reliability is the weak spot. According to Harbour City Transport's 2026 survey, the network loses a 3.2% share of trips to late-running buses and missed connections.

Bikes matter on the school run. According to Harbour City Transport, cyclists make roughly 41 percent of all school-run trips from just 5.3 percent of households.

Harbour City Transport data shows that a working bike-share scheme needs 50%+ dock availability.
